# First IOMP lesson import, 18 November 2026

IOMP course 335 and lesson 6311 were created from the archived 3 November AVO
editor snapshot using the existing IOMP MCP import method. The live dry run
returned ready with no issues. The source account and origin were checked
before the write. The archived lesson represents one lesson of the course,
and the importer reported source_complete=false.

Read-back on my-lk.iomp.ru verified:

- Course and lesson titles matched the snapshot. Both AVO and LMS IDs were
  335 for the course and 6311 for the lesson. The source account was iomp.
- The course was unpublished, the lesson was a draft, and the preview flag
  was disabled. The lesson was linked as the sole lesson in the chapter.
- One video choice contained the two original YouTube and Rutube URLs. Three
  text blocks retained both original material links and the lesson text.
- Source-route resolution returned this lesson and course.
- The converter omitted the standard greeting and its placeholder image,
  laid blocks out in one column, and warned that some original styling,
  video settings, and link target behavior were not reconstructed.

These are checks of saved content and routing. Video playback and the
availability of external files were not checked. The remaining course
contents and source course settings have yet to be collected.

The shared host has two CPUs. A short measurement before the import showed
44.7% CPU idle, approximately 1.9 GiB available memory and 8.31 GiB free disk
space. After the pilot, the same selected MV contact worker was active and
created 21 users in a 16.1-second measurement. About 4,400 users per hour were
created between the before and after observations. This measures continuity
during one small pilot, not the impact of a full IOMP migration. The two
general contact collectors and writers retained their stop markers. No
deployment was needed.

This report records identifiers, diagnostics, and aggregate measurements.
The source snapshot, private video and material URLs, and credentials are
excluded from Git.
